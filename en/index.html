What is coffee, exactly?

Green coffee, roasted coffee, cascara, Café Intégral, Torrégral: all these words refer to the same fruit, but not to the same part, nor to the same level of transformation.

Green coffeeThe unroasted bean, before aromatic transformation.
Roasted coffeeThe familiar taste of coffee: warm, dark, stimulating.
CascaraThe dried skin of the cherry, soft and fruity.
Café IntégralThe whole cherry, approached as the original experience of the fruit.
TorrégralThe bridge: coffee taste, a more complete view of the coffee tree.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Is it still coffee?

Yes. Torrégral is real coffee, simply approached in a more complete way. No ingredient foreign to coffee is needed to understand its value.

Is it a coffee substitute?

No. It is not fake coffee. It is another way to drink coffee, with more respect for the cherry it comes from.

Is it a supplement?

No. Torrégral is a food beverage, a coffee. It is not presented as a treatment or as a medical supplement.

How do I prepare it?

Like your usual coffee: filter coffee maker, French press, espresso or slow methods. The ritual stays simple.

Does it contain less caffeine?

The essential difference is not caffeine alone, but the balance sought with the compounds of the coffee fruit.
